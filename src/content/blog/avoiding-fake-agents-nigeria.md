Property scams in Nigeria tend to follow familiar patterns. Knowing them in advance is the single best protection you have.

## Red flags to watch for

- Pressure to pay immediately "before someone else takes it"
- Refusal to let you visit the property in person before paying anything
- A price significantly below similar listings nearby, with no clear reason why
- No willingness to provide a survey plan, deed, or C of O for inspection

## Documents worth verifying before you pay anything

For land or property purchases specifically, ask to see:

- Survey plan, and confirm it matches the actual plot boundaries on the ground
- Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) or Governor's Consent
- Evidence of a search at the relevant land registry confirming no encumbrances

## A simple rule that saves most people

Never transfer money to an individual's personal account for a property you haven't physically inspected. Legitimate transactions can withstand a short delay for you to verify documents — pressure to skip that step is the clearest scam signal there is.

If something feels rushed, it usually is.
