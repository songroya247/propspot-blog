Bodija is one of Ibadan's more established residential areas, and it shows in the small details — steadier road maintenance, an active market, and a mix of long-term residents alongside newer arrivals.

## What it's actually like day-to-day

Bodija market is the anchor of the area, and it means fresh produce and everyday goods are rarely more than a short walk away. Traffic around the market can get heavy in the mornings, so factor that into any commute estimate.

## Who it suits

- Families who want an established, settled feel rather than a newer developing estate
- Anyone who values walkability to a major market
- Tenants looking for a range of housing types, from self-contains to full duplexes

## What to budget for

Rental prices in Bodija sit in the mid-to-upper range for Ibadan, reflecting its established status. Expect one to two years upfront, plus standard agency and agreement fees.

## Getting around

Bodija connects reasonably well to UI, Sango, and the wider Ibadan road network, making it a workable base whether you commute toward the university side of town or further into the city center.
